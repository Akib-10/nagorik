How Nagorik works

Three steps between a broken road and a fixed one. In that order, every time.

STEP 01

Spot & snap

See a pothole, a dark street, an overflowing drain? Take a photo, drop a pin on the map, and write a quick description.

STEP 02

Neighbours confirm

Others upvote issues they've seen too. The more confirmations, the louder the signal to authorities.

STEP 03

Authorities act

High-priority issues auto-notify the relevant city office. Track status live — from "Reported" to "Fixed".

Built to actually get things done

Not just a complaint box. A real system that connects citizens to authorities.

Photo & video reports

Snap it, upload it. Visual evidence makes issues impossible to ignore.

Live hotspot tracking

See every civic problem in your area on one feed. Priorities become obvious.

Community voting

Upvote issues you've seen too. The most-voted problems automatically escalate.

Authority alerts

High-engagement issues ping the relevant city offices and utility teams directly.
